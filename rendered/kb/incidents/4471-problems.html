  • Parser. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant’s dates come out flipped: 7 April becomes 4 July. This affects every tenant, through the app, email and the booking-v2 form. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • Form hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form then reads it the tenant’s way. The result looks the same as the parser bug, but it comes only through the form on booking-v3 tenants. A parser fix does not touch it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired.
  • One-day moves. A date moves by a day, or differs between two people for the same meeting. Swapping day and month cannot turn 12 May into 11 May, so the parser does not explain these. We do not have the cause yet. The Lisbon and Toronto report points at time zones, but that is a lead, not a finding. Open: the engineering update does not cover these.
The parser fix does not settle the one-day moves. If a report of a one-day move comes in, add it to the open ticket. Do not read it as the parser bug coming back.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges reports on subject alone, and nobody reads the body. Reporters describe the symptom, and all three problems have the same symptom: the wrong day. So the same subjects appear across them, and subject matching cannot separate them.

How to check this

  • Count by report id. The export repeats some reports across its pages.
  • Use the tenant directory for the locale and form, not the browser locale on the report.
  • The one-day list is in desk-notes.json. Read those reports yourself: a few of them are thin, and more reports could move the picture.
